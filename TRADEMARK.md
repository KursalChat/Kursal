These are "all rights reserved." Copying the code does not copy permission to use them.

## What you may do

- Fork, modify, and redistribute the code under the AGPL-3.0, as the license allows
- Say that your project is **based on**, **derived from**, or **compatible with** Kursal
- Use the name in ordinary descriptive speech: articles, reviews, comparisons, documentation, talks
- Reproduce the logo when referring to the Kursal project itself, unmodified

## What you may not do

- Name your fork or product "Kursal", or a name close enough to be confused with it
- Use the logo, icon, or wordmark as the identity of your fork or product
- Use the name or marks in a way that suggests we publish, endorse, maintain, or vouch for your version
- Register the name or a confusingly similar one as a trademark, domain, or app store listing

If you distribute a modified build, give it your own name and your own icon. Users need to be able to tell whose build they are running.

## Asking

If you want to do something this file does not cover, or you think your case is reasonable and it says no, write to <hello@kursal.chat>. We are not trying to be difficult, and we would rather say yes to something specific than write a broader rule.
